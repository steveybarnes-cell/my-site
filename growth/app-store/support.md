# Support — MPG Site Records

Need help with MPG Site Records? Our support team is here to assist tradesmen, site managers, and admins with any questions or issues. Please read the sections below before getting in touch — many common questions can be resolved quickly with the right information.

## How To Reach Support

You can contact the MPG Site Records support team by email or phone:

- Email: info@my-project.co.uk
- Phone: 0044 0117 251 05 65
- Email is preferred for account and data queries as it allows us to respond with a written record. We aim to respond to email enquiries within two business days.

## What To Include In Your Request

To help us resolve your issue as quickly as possible, please include the following details when you contact us:

- Your full name and the email address registered to your account.
- Your role in the App: tradesman, site manager, or admin.
- A clear description of the problem, including what you were trying to do and what happened instead.
- The iOS version on your device and the version of MPG Site Records you are using (found in Settings within the App).
- Screenshots or screen recordings if the issue involves something visual.
- The approximate date and time the issue occurred.

## Account And Access Issues

If you are unable to sign in, have forgotten your credentials, or believe your account has been set up incorrectly, please contact support with your registered email address. For security reasons we may ask you to verify your identity before making changes to your account.

If you need your account to be deleted and your personal data removed, email us at info@my-project.co.uk with the subject line 'Account Deletion Request'. We will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

## Privacy And Data Requests

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct inaccurate data, or to request deletion of your data. To submit any of these requests, please email info@my-project.co.uk with the subject line 'Data Request' and include your registered email address and the nature of your request.

If you have concerns about how your data is being used, please review our Privacy Policy or contact us directly. We will acknowledge your request and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

## Response Expectations

We aim to respond to all support enquiries within two business days. Complex account or data requests may take longer. We will keep you informed of progress if additional time is needed.

Please note that support is provided in English. Out-of-hours messages will be picked up on the next available business day.
