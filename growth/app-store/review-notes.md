# Review Notes

- MPG Site Records is a closed, invite-only business tool used by My Project Group Limited and its authorised subcontractors. New accounts cannot be self-registered; they are provisioned by an admin.
- A demo account has been prepared for App Review. Please use the credentials provided in the demo account checklist below.
- The app requests location permission (when in use only) to confirm the user is on or near an allocated site at the moment of clock-in, clock-out, or evidence upload. Location is never accessed in the background.
- The microphone and speech recognition permissions are used together for on-device dictation of daily site records. No audio is stored or transmitted.
- The camera permission is used to photograph site progress and capture receipts. Photos are timestamped and attached to the relevant job record.
- Photo library read permission allows attaching existing photos and documents to jobs. Photo library write permission saves captured site photos back to the library.
- OpenAI API integration is used to assist with intelligent feature processing within the app. No personally identifying data beyond what is necessary for the feature is transmitted.
- The app contains user-generated content (site records, photos, receipts) visible only to the submitting tradesman and authorised admins and site managers within the same organisation. There is no public-facing content feed.
