# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MPG Site Records collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our iOS application. Please read it carefully. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated automatically as you use the app.

- Account information: when you register or sign in we collect your email address and a user identifier to manage your account.
- Profile data: trade, business details, and compliance information such as UTR, NI number, CIS status, and VAT registration that you enter in the app.
- Daily site records: start and finish times, breaks, work descriptions, delays, and calculated hours you submit.
- Photos and files: images you capture or select from your photo library, linked to jobs and timestamped.
- Materials and receipts: descriptions and photographs of materials purchased and uploaded against a job.
- Location data: GPS coordinates collected when you clock in or out using the geofenced attendance feature.
- Usage data: interactions with app features, screens visited, and actions taken, collected through our analytics integration.
- Diagnostic data: crash reports and performance information to help us identify and fix issues.

## How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve MPG Site Records.

- To authenticate your account and provide role-appropriate access (Tradesman, Site Manager, Admin).
- To record, store, and display your daily site records, timesheets, and invoices.
- To associate photos, receipts, and materials with the correct job.
- To verify attendance through GPS geofencing at clock-in and clock-out.
- To enable admins and site managers to review, query, approve, and process payments.
- To analyse how features are used so we can improve the app.
- To detect and address technical errors and crashes.
- To communicate with you about your account or support requests.

## Device Permissions And Sensitive Data

MPG Site Records requests the following device permissions. You can manage these in your iOS Settings at any time.

- Photo Library: required to let you select existing photos of site work, materials, and receipts from your device.
- Location (When In Use): required to record your GPS position when you clock in or out of a geofenced site. Location is not collected in the background.
- We do not access your camera directly, microphone, contacts, or health data.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only as described below.

- Within the organisation: your site records, timesheets, invoices, and photos are visible to authorised admins and site managers within the My Project Group Ltd system.
- Service providers: we use a custom backend API to store and process data. We may use third-party analytics services to understand app usage. These providers are permitted to use data only to provide their services to us.
- Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process.
- Business transfers: if the organisation or its assets are transferred, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## Data Retention

We retain your account and job-related data for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Photos and files uploaded to the platform are retained until you or an administrator removes them or the account is closed.

## Your Choices

You can update your profile information from within the app at any time. If you wish to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held about you, please contact us using the details in the Contact section below. Note that some data may need to be retained for legal or contractual reasons.

## Children's Privacy

MPG Site Records is designed for use by construction industry professionals and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

## Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact the team responsible for MPG Site Records. No public contact email or website URL has been provided at this time — please refer to the support information distributed with the app.
