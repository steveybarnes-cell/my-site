# Privacy Policy — MPG Site Records

This Privacy Policy explains how My Project Group Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the MPG Site Records application ("the App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at info@my-project.co.uk.

## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated by your use of the App.

- Account information: your name, email address, trade, business details, and compliance information such as UTR number, National Insurance number, CIS status, and VAT registration.
- Daily site records: start and finish times, break durations, work descriptions, delays, and calculated hours.
- Photos: timestamped images taken with your device camera or selected from your photo library, categorised as before, during, or after works.
- Materials and receipts: descriptions, costs, and photographs of supplier receipts submitted against a job.
- Location data: a single location reading captured at the moment you clock in, clock out, or upload site evidence — used solely to confirm proximity to the allocated site. Location is not tracked continuously in the background.
- Invoice and payment data: hours worked, net amounts due, and CIS deduction records.
- Device and usage data: app interaction events and crash diagnostics collected via analytics services to help us improve the App.

## How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes.

- To provide and operate the App, including authenticating your account and synchronising records between your device and our servers.
- To allow site managers and administrators to review, query, approve, and pay submitted timesheets and invoices.
- To confirm site attendance through GPS-verified clock-in and clock-out.
- To generate and display weekly invoices showing hours, net due, and CIS deductions.
- To send alerts about missing receipts or late invoice submissions.
- To improve App performance and reliability using aggregated analytics and crash-diagnostic data.
- To comply with applicable legal and contractual obligations.

## Device Permissions And Sensitive Data

The App requests the following device permissions. You may manage these in your iOS Settings at any time; revoking a permission will limit the relevant App functionality.

- Camera: used to photograph work progress and capture supplier receipts on site.
- Photo Library (read): used to attach existing photos and receipts from your device library to a job or daily record.
- Photo Library (add): used to save site photos you capture in the App back to your device library.
- Location (when in use): a single reading taken only when you clock in, clock out, or submit site evidence. Location is not accessed in the background.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances.

- Within your organisation: information you submit is visible to the administrators and site managers of My Project Group Ltd who are responsible for reviewing and approving your records.
- Service providers: we use Supabase to host and authenticate account data, and analytics tools to monitor App performance. These providers process data on our behalf under appropriate data-processing arrangements.
- OpenAI: certain features may use OpenAI's API to process text you submit within the App. No personally identifiable financial or compliance data is intentionally sent to OpenAI.
- Legal obligations: we may disclose information where required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request.

## Data Retention

We retain your account and job records for as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfil our legal and contractual obligations, including CIS and construction industry record-keeping requirements.

If you wish to request deletion of your data, please contact us at info@my-project.co.uk. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and advise you of any records we are required to retain by law.

## Your Choices

You may update your profile information within the App at any time. You may also contact us at info@my-project.co.uk to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal retention obligations.

You can manage device permissions (camera, photo library, location) in iOS Settings. Revoking permissions will disable the corresponding App features but will not affect previously submitted records.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by construction industry tradesmen, subcontractors, site managers, and administrators. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
